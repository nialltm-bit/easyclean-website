Cleaned at your home

We always clean your rug at your home. We check the fabric and test for colourfastness, pre-treat, then clean with hot water extraction and groom the pile.

The products we use are safe for pets and children. We still ask you to keep everyone off the rug until it’s dry. We give you a drying time before we leave.

Wool, silk, antique or fringed rugs

Wool, silk, antique and heavily fringed rugs carry a real risk of shrinkage or colour bleed from wet cleaning. If you have one, book as normal. We’ll get in touch after you book to talk it through, and we may recommend against cleaning it rather than risk damage. See our terms for what isn’t covered.
