About Keynsham

Keynsham is a town with a lot of newer homes. Somerdale is about 700 Taylor Wimpey homes, including blocks of flats, on the former Cadbury factory site. The 1930s factory buildings became a retirement community.

Hygge Park is a Crest Nicholson estate of around 250 homes, first launched in 2019, from two-bed flats to five-bed houses. It has its own primary school, Two Rivers. A new garden community of around 1,500 homes is planned at North Keynsham.

That means a mix of young families, people who commute to Bath and Bristol, and retirement flats. Flats and houses are priced the same way here: by room, stairs and item, with the total shown before you book.
