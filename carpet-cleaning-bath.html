About Bath

Bath is mostly Georgian townhouses in the centre, and many of them are split into flats or shared houses. A small flat and a large Georgian house conversion are very different jobs, which is why we price by room and item and show the price before you book.

Oldfield Park is the student area, with more shared student houses than anywhere else in Bath. Almost all of them are Victorian and Edwardian terraces, not purpose-built blocks. Bath is hilly, so the housing changes a lot from one part of the city to the next. Oldfield Park itself is flat.

Student tenancies there mostly run for 12 months from around July or August, so end-of-tenancy cleans tend to bunch up in the summer. If you are a landlord or agent, see our end-of-tenancy cleaning and landlord and agent page. You can arrange access with us, so you do not need to be there.

Online bookings need at least 24 hours’ notice, and you can book up to 21 days ahead. For anything sooner, WhatsApp us. Cleans booked online can start between 8am and 6pm, Monday to Saturday.
