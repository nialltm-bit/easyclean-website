About Frome

Frome is known for its independent shops and cobbled streets. Trinity is a planned grid of workers’ houses from the late 1600s and early 1700s, built on wool money. About half was pulled down in the 1960s, and the rest was saved as a rare example of early industrial housing.

Older homes come in all shapes and sizes. Our room prices go by size, and you see the total before you book. If you are not sure which size fits, send a couple of photos on WhatsApp for a Quick Quote and we will come back with a firm price the same day.
