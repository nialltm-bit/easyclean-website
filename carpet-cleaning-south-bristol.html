About South Bristol

Bedminster and Southville are full of bay-windowed Victorian terraces, often compared to Clifton but cheaper. They attract young professionals, families and creatives. Brislington is mostly Victorian and early-1900s houses.

Further out, Hartcliffe is a post-war estate, mostly council houses, on the northern slopes of Dundry Hill. Hengrove, Hartcliffe and Withywood were 20th-century estates that greatly expanded south Bristol.

We also cover the centre and east of Bristol. Homes here range from Victorian terraces to post-war estates, and the price is fixed by room or item and shown before you book.
